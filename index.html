Personalized Launch Preparation Map

Three tracks, one manageable next move.

This is a flexible map rather than a rigid checklist. Real answers and experience decide what belongs next.

1

Safe starting service

Choose dog count, walk length, availability, service area, supplies, and a meet-and-greet routine. Start with one household at a time rather than unfamiliar pack walks.

First evidence: a service you can confidently explain and perform.
2

Easier-to-find marketing

Describe the best-fit client, write one clear promise, and test small messages where local dog owners already look and talk.

First evidence: inquiries from people who understand the offer.
3

Five recurring relationships

When the business is ready, turn good first walks into trust, repeat scheduling, feedback, and referrals. Add employee capacity only after demand clarifies the help needed.

Future launch aim: five regular clients and an honest profitability measure.

03 Prove Show you can repeat operations and understand the numbers.
04 Launch Replace simulation pieces with real-world accounts and customers.
